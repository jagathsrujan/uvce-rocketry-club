# Timeline and Logistics

**The 5-week run sheet.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Total duration** | 5 weeks from announcement to core team |
| **Your time** | ~25–30 hours across the whole drive |
| **Helpers needed** | 2–3, especially for the doubt session and interviews |

## Master checklist

### Before you start
- [ ] Faculty advisor confirmed
- [ ] Reader finalised — [`03_Foundation_Reader.md`](<03_Foundation_Reader.md>)
- [ ] Test and answer key finalised — [`04_Open_Book_Test.md`](<04_Open_Book_Test.md>)
- [ ] Two other people willing to help assess
- [ ] WhatsApp broadcast group created (admins only can post)
- [ ] Shared folder for submissions
- [ ] 🔴 **Decided what general members get** — see the warning in [`README.md`](<README.md>)

### Week 1
- [ ] Sign-up form live (4 fields max)
- [ ] Announcement posted once to each group
- [ ] Classroom permissions obtained
- [ ] Posters up with QR codes
- [ ] Reminder sent on Day 5
- [ ] Form closed Day 7, video list sent

### Week 2
- [ ] Room booked for doubt session
- [ ] Demo materials prepared (water rocket / OpenRocket / inert grain)
- [ ] Reminder sent 24 h before
- [ ] **Doubt session run**
- [ ] Attendance recorded
- [ ] Reader + test + essay sent the same evening
- [ ] Engagement notes written

### Week 3
- [ ] Reminder sent Day 18
- [ ] **Test and essay collected**
- [ ] Marked by two people independently
- [ ] Rankings produced

### Week 4
- [ ] Interview shortlist sent
- [ ] **Interviews completed**
- [ ] Scores reconciled same day
- [ ] Decision meeting held
- [ ] 🔴 **All outcomes communicated within 48 hours**

### Week 5
- [ ] First core meeting, roles assigned
- [ ] Learning paths distributed
- [ ] Marvel enrolments and applications submitted
- [ ] 🔴 **Safety induction completed**
- [ ] Strand burner build started

## What can go wrong

| Problem | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Low sign-ups** | Announcement too quiet, or too demanding | Push classroom announcements harder; simplify the ask |
| **Good sign-ups, poor doubt session attendance** | Reminder not sent, bad timing, wrong room | Remind 24 h before; pick late afternoon; confirm the room the day before |
| **Nobody does the test** | Deadline unclear, or the reader felt too hard | Announce the deadline at the session; make sure the reader is genuinely readable |
| **Everyone scores well** | The test was too easy | Fine for the first intake — rely on the interview to differentiate |
| **Everyone scores badly** | The reader was too dense, or they did not read | Check whether the *safety* question was answered well; consider a second intake with a gentler reader |
| **Strong candidate, failed Section E** | Safety disposition | 🔴 **Interview them regardless of total score.** See [`04_Open_Book_Test.md`](<04_Open_Book_Test.md). |
| **You end up doing everything alone** | The core problem this whole process exists to fix | Recruit one helper *before* you start. You cannot run Stage 3 alone. |
| **People drop out in month two** | Normal | Keep the first commitment small; give every member something to finish this week |

---

## The one thing to protect

> **Send every outcome, to everyone, on time.**

It costs you twenty minutes and it is the difference between a club that people trust and one they
warn others about. In a college you will be in for years, your reputation for how you treat applicants
travels faster than your reputation for building rockets.

## After this recruitment drive

Once the core team exists:

1. **Move to the ongoing training** — [`../90_LEARNING/`](<../90_LEARNING/README.md>)
2. **Assign subsystem ownership** — two people minimum per subsystem
3. **Build the strand burner** — the critical path
4. **Set a fixed weekly meeting time** and keep it
5. **Start the progress log** in [`../README.md`](<../README.md>) — this is the club's memory

**Then run this same funnel again next semester.** It gets easier every time, and it is how the club
outlives you.
