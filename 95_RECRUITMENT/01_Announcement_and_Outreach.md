# Stage 1 — Announcement and Outreach

**Getting the message in front of people who might care.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **When** | Week 1 |
| **Goal** | Collect names and contact details. **Nothing else.** |
| **Output** | A sign-up list |

---

## The rule for this stage

> **Ask for the smallest possible commitment.** Not membership. Not attendance. Just: *"watch some
> videos and tell me what you think."*

Every extra thing you ask for at this stage costs you people. The funnel gets harder later — this
stage should be almost effortless to say yes to.

---

## Where to post

| Channel | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **WhatsApp** | Department groups, batch groups, hostel groups | Where Indian student communication actually happens. Ask a friend in each group to post if you are not a member. |
| **Classroom announcements** | 3 minutes at the start or end of a lecture, in each branch | 🔴 **Most effective single method for 1st/2nd years.** Ask a friendly faculty member for permission. **Do not overrun the three minutes.** |
| **Notice boards** | Every branch, with a QR code | Cheap, persistent |
| **Instagram / LinkedIn** | Club account, and personal posts | Reaches people who are not in your groups |
| **Word of mouth** | Tell everyone you know, individually | Underrated. A personal ask converts far better than a poster. |

> ⚠️ **Do not spam.** One post per group, plus a single reminder. Being the person who floods group
> chats costs you goodwill you will need later.

---

## The announcement message

Keep it short. Long messages do not get read.

> **Starting a rocketry club at UVCE. Want in?**
>
> We are building and flying real rockets — designing our own solid motors, casting propellant,
> building flight computers, and testing on a private site outside Bengaluru.
>
> No experience needed. Genuinely none. Nobody at UVCE knows how to do this yet, including us.
>
> **First step is easy:** watch a couple of hours of videos (we will send a list) and tell us what you
> think. That is it.
>
> If you are curious, drop your name and number here: [form link]
>
> — [Your name], [branch], [semester]

### Why this message works

| Element | Purpose |
|---|---|
| "real rockets" | Concrete, not aspirational |
| "no experience needed" | Removes the main reason people self-exclude |
| "nobody knows yet, including us" | **Honest.** Also disarming — it says you are not gatekeeping. |
| "first step is easy" | The actual ask is tiny |
| Named contact | A person, not an anonymous club |

---

## The sign-up form

Keep it to four fields. Every additional field loses people.

| Field | Why |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Obviously |
| **WhatsApp number** | The only contact method that works |
| **Branch and semester** | For planning, and to weight toward 1st/2nd years |
| **"What made you curious?"** (optional, one line) | Free signal, and it gives you something to say at the doubt session |

**Do not ask for:** CGPA, prior experience, skills, or a CV. You are not hiring.

---

## Who to target

| Year | Priority | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **1st year** | 🔴 **Highest** | Four years of runway. They become the club's memory. |
| **2nd year** | 🔴 **Highest** | Three years, and mature enough to be trusted with hardware soon |
| **3rd year** | Medium | Useful capability now, but do not build succession on them |
| **4th year** | Low for members; **high as mentors** | Their time is gone. Recruit as alumni mentors instead. |

**All branches welcome.** Say so explicitly. If the club becomes mechanical-only, the avionics will
never mature — and avionics is the largest single workload.

| Branch | What they typically own |
|---|---|
| Mechanical | Structures, propulsion hardware, mechanisms, fabrication |
| ECE / EEE | Flight computer, sensors, power, RF |
| CSE / ISE | Flight software, ground station, data analysis |
| **Any branch** | Operations, documentation, sponsorship, design |

---

## Set expectations honestly at Stage 1

Tell them up front:

- **This is a real time commitment** — roughly 6–8 hours a week for core members
- **It involves energetic materials** and strict safety rules that are not negotiable
- **Not everyone will make the core team** — but everyone who applies gets access to the workshops
  and learning material
- **There is a selection process**, and it is about follow-through, not prior knowledge

> 🔴 **The last point matters most.** Telling people that trying does not mean being discarded is what
> lets them commit. See the warning in the [master plan](<README.md>).

## Suggested announcement timeline

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Post to WhatsApp groups + social media. Open the form. |
| **Day 2–3** | Classroom announcements (3 min each, all branches) |
| **Day 3** | Notice boards with QR codes |
| **Day 5** | Single reminder post |
| **Day 7** | Close the form. Send the video list to everyone who signed up. |

## What "success" looks like

**50–100 sign-ups from a college of UVCE's size is a good result.** Do not be disappointed by
anything above 30. If you get 200, great — but plan for the fact that most will not reach Stage 3.

**The number that actually matters is how many turn up in person at the doubt session.** That is the
first real signal.
