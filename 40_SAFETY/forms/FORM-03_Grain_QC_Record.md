## 5. CHECK 2 — COIN TAP TEST

**Applicability:** only for a grain with an **integral casting tube acting as the inhibitor**. The
inhibitor **must** be intimately bonded — any disbonding lets hot gas seep between the inhibitor and the
propellant, **increasing the burning area** and over-pressurising the motor.

> ### 🔴 THE TAP TEST IS ONLY VALID AFTER FULL CURE
> Performed too early, **disbonding indications may be false**. Residual moisture in the bondline can
> cause a false failure; a few days in a desiccator often converts a fail to a pass.

**Grain fully cured?** ☐ Yes ☐ No — **if No, STOP: the tap test result is not valid. Reschedule it.**

### 5.2 The critical rule — batch-relative comparison

> ### 🔴 WHAT TO ACTUALLY WATCH FOR
> **A DIFFERENT TAP RESPONSE *BETWEEN GRAIN SEGMENTS OF THE SAME BATCH*.**
> If one segment sounds distinctly different and less solid than its batch-mates, its inhibitor bond may
> be flawed. **REJECT THAT SEGMENT.**
>
> - A **single isolated odd spot** is generally **not** a problem.
> - **A different tap sound at the seam of a rolled casting tube is NORMAL.**
> - ⚠️ **The difference is hard to describe in words and takes experience to recognise. Practise on
>   known-good grains.**

## 7. Pre-firing preparation

### 7.1 Residual grease film removal

> The coring rod and mould are lubricated with silicone grease. **That grease film must be cleaned off
> the grain before the primer is applied** — a primer will not bond to a greasy surface, and a poorly
> bonded primer causes delayed, uneven ignition.

| **Solvent stored and used away from all ignition sources, with ventilation** | ☐ Yes ☐ No |
| **Solvent rags disposed of in a labelled sealed container, not in general waste** | ☐ Yes ☐ No |

**Grease film cleaned with:** ☐ **Acetone** ☐ **Lacquer thinner** — tick the one actually used.

### 7.2 Ignition primer coat

> Bare KNSB is **reluctant to ignite**, so a **thin** coat of ignition primer on the exposed grain
> surfaces — the **core and both end faces** — is required.
>
> ⚠️ **A HEAVY coat defeats the purpose and causes DELAYED IGNITION. Thin means thin.**
>
> ⚠️ **Perchlorate-based pyrolant coatings are prohibited to this club** (`Safety_Manual_Master.md`
> §3.4). A nitrate-based primer must be researched, authorised, and tested before use. **Until then,
> grains are fired unprimed**, with an appropriately sized igniter and the expectation of a slower,
> mountain-shaped pressure curve.

## 10. Quick reference — the whole decision in six lines

1. Weigh the grain, measure its dimensions, compute **density = mass ÷ volume**.
2. Compare against the **ideal 1.841 g/cm³**.
3. About **95% of ideal is typical for good casting**. About **90% or below indicates significant air inclusion**.
4. **External bubbles may be repaired** by opening the cavity and filling with molten propellant.
5. **Below 90% with no visible external flaws → the grain MUST be discarded** — firing it risks a **CATO**
   from unpredictable burning-rate behaviour.
6. **Tap test:** a grain that **sounds different from its batch-mates is rejected**. The tap test is only
   valid **after full cure**.
