# UVCE Rocketry Club — Printable Operational Forms

**Folder:** `40_SAFETY/forms/`
**Parent documents:** `40_SAFETY/Safety_Manual_Master.md` and its four subordinate procedures
**Issuing authority:** UVCE Rocketry Club Safety Committee
**Status:** Controlled document. Changes only via the Safety Officer with Safety Committee approval.
**Review cycle:** every 6 months, and immediately after any incident, near miss, or procedure change.

> **These forms are the field-carryable face of the safety documents.** The checklists in the parent
> procedures are written for reading; these sheets are written for **signing at the bench, at the pad,
> and on the range.** Every form is self-contained: if you have the sheet, you have the checklist, the
> limits, the emergency numbers, and the sign-off block.

## 2. How to fill these in

1. **Print before you travel.** These are field forms. A form on a laptop at home is not a form.
2. **Fill in the header block first**, before any work starts — site, date, names, roles.
3. **One tick per box, in ink.** A tick means *you personally verified it*, not that someone said it
   was fine.
4. **Write the actual number in the blank.** "OK" is not a measurement. An estimated wind is not a
   measurement. If there is no instrument, the item fails.
5. **Record a failure as a failure.** A sheet with a `FAIL` and a stop is a good sheet. A sheet with
   everything ticked and a shortcut taken behind it is a disciplinary matter
   (`Safety_Manual_Master.md` §9).
6. **Nobody signs for work they did not witness.** Signature blocks name a role *and* a person.
7. **Any single NO-GO / FAIL stops the session.** There is no discussion, no vote, and no override.
8. **The forms are not a substitute for the procedures.** Read the parent procedure end-to-end before
   every energetic operation. Do not work from memory.

## 3. Printing guidance

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Paper** | **A4, white.** Use the printer's "fit to page" setting; the sheets are laid out to a single page wherever possible. |
| **Sides** | **Print single-sided.** A double-sided sheet cannot be laid flat on a clipboard without losing the reverse, cannot be signed one-handed, and cannot be photographed for the record without turning it over. |
| **Copies** | **One form per test, per session, per grain, per flight day.** Do not photocopy a used sheet to reuse it — a pre-ticked box is a falsified record. |
| **Clipboard** | Standard A4 clipboard, with a pen attached to the board. **Pen, not pencil** — pencil fades and can be altered. |
| **Weather** | It is windy and it may rain. Carry the sheet in a clear plastic sleeve or a clip case, and write with a permanent-ink pen. A wet sheet is an unreadable record. |
| **Legibility** | Print in black on white. Do not print in colour-on-colour; these are read in bright sun and in poor light. |
| **Quantity** | Carry **at least two blank copies of FORM-01 and FORM-06** to every session — one to use, one spare, and the incident form may be needed without warning. |
| **Emergency numbers** | Confirm that **112 (all emergencies), 108 (ambulance), and 101 (fire)** are pre-printed and legible on the sheet you are carrying. Never rely on a phone that may be flat. |

**Do not print these forms at reduced size.** The tick boxes exist to be ticked with a pen by a person
wearing gloves, in wind, in a hurry.

## 4. Records retention — mandatory

- **Retain every completed form for the club records for at least 3 years.** This is the minimum; the
  following categories are retained **permanently** because they outlive any individual member's time
  in the club:
  - **Incident and near-miss reports (FORM-06) — permanent**, with a copy to the faculty advisor
    (`Safety_Manual_Master.md` §8.4).
  - **Completed static test checklists (FORM-04)** — filed with the test report, permanent.
  - **Casting and grain QC records (FORM-02, FORM-03)** — permanent, because a grain may be fired
    years after it was cast and its record must still exist.
- **Everything else (FORM-01, FORM-05) — minimum 3 years**, and permanent where the session produced a
  test report.
- **Storage:** a physical ring binder per academic year in the club's filing cabinet, **and** a scanned
  PDF copy in the club's shared drive under `40_SAFETY/forms/completed/<YYYY>/`.
- **Scanning:** photograph or scan the completed sheet **on the same day**, while the ink is fresh and
  the sheet is not yet coffee-stained. Name the scan
  `<form-number>_<YYYY-MM-DD>_<site-or-batch>_<initials>.pdf`.
- **Never destroy an incident or near-miss report.** These survive member graduation — that is the
  point.
- **Records are handed over** at every office-bearer transition, with a written inventory of what was
  handed over and to whom.

## 5. Form control

| Field in the header | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Form** | The controlled form number, e.g. `FORM-04`. |
| **Revision** | The revision of this sheet. **A revision change invalidates old printed stock** — destroy superseded paper copies. |
| **Issued** | The date this revision was issued by the Safety Committee. |
| **Sheet no. / of** | For multi-page forms, and to make clear that a second sheet exists. |
| **Session / Test / Batch ID** | The unique identifier that ties this sheet to the test log, the batch record, and the data files. **Never leave this blank.** |
