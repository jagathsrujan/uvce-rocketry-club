## 4. Section B — The three-item rule (mandatory whenever propellant is present)

> **Nakka safety rule 3.** Three items must **ALWAYS** be present **before propellant is uncovered** —
> not fetched afterwards. An empty or half-full dunk bucket stops the session.

| Item | Standard | Verified by | Pass / Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Dunk bucket** | Full, clean water, **≥ 20 L**, **within 2 m** of the workstation, deep enough to submerge a hand and forearm | ____________ | ☐ Pass ☐ **FAIL** |

| **3. Escape route** | **Physically walked**, clear, known to everyone present, muster point agreed and upwind | ____________ | ☐ Pass ☐ **FAIL** |

## 5. Section C — People

- [ ] **Minimum two authorised persons present.** No one will be alone with propellant, oxidiser, mixed powder, molten propellant, ignition powder, or a loaded motor at any time
- [ ] Everyone present is **fit for duty** — not tired (≥ 6 h sleep, ≤ 10 h on feet), not rushed, no illness, no hand injury affecting grip, no significant emotional distress
- [ ] **No alcohol** in anyone's system within 12 hours; none on site at any time
- [ ] No recreational drugs; any impairing prescription medication disclosed to the Safety Officer
- [ ] Everyone's **PPE** checked and correct — chemical splash goggles, face shield (where molten propellant is poured or a motor is approached after firing), heat-resistant gloves, cotton or **FR** clothing with full sleeves and full-length trousers, closed shoes
- [ ] **NO SYNTHETIC CLOTHING** near propellant — no nylon, polyester, rayon, fleece, or synthetic blends, including inner layers and socks
- [ ] Leather aprons available for casting; dust masks available for grinding and sifting
- [ ] Rings, watches, bracelets removed; hair tied back
- [ ] Everyone knows the **emergency numbers** (112 / 108 / 101), the **muster point**, and the **written location description**
- [ ] Everyone can hear a shouted **"ABORT"**, **"STOP"**, or **"FIRE"** — or a horn/whistle/radio is in use
- [ ] **Phones are away** from the work area; the documentarian's fixed position has been briefed
- [ ] Every person present has been briefed on: hazards, sequence of operations, abort signals, escape routes, muster point, and their own role

## 11. NO-GO conditions — the quick reference (any one stops the session)

1. Any person downrange, in the blast fan, or inside the exclusion zone
2. A member of the public, a vehicle, or livestock inside the minimum safe distance
3. Wind above the limit for the activity (propellant work 25 km/h · static test sustained 20 / gusts 25 km/h · flight sustained 32 / gusts +10 km/h)
4. Lightning within 10 km, thunder in the last 30 minutes, or a thunderstorm approaching
5. Rain, or wet ground at the pad
6. **Dunk bucket not full**, or extinguisher out of date, unpressurised, or the wrong type
7. Escape route blocked, unclear, or not walked and known by everyone
8. Fewer than the required number of people — **never a solo test, never a solo launch**
9. No Safety Officer present, or no Test Director / session lead named
10. Grain has not passed the density check and the tap test
11. Igniter leads not shunted, or shunting discipline not verifiable
12. Ignition box shows current at the igniter terminals with the box in SAFE
13. Igniter leads un-shunted when they should be shunted
14. Any person on site who has not been briefed on the sequence, abort signals, escape routes, and muster point
15. Any person on site without the required PPE
16. Phones in hand during an energetic operation
17. Any person unfit for duty — fatigued, ill, impaired, or within 12 hours of alcohol
18. Lighting inadequate, or less than 90 minutes of daylight remaining
19. Any unexplained anomaly — a measurement that does not match, a component that does not look right, a change nobody can account for
20. **Schedule pressure being used as a reason to proceed**

> **Item 20 causes all the others.** If you are rushing, stop the session. A delayed session costs a
> weekend. A rushed session costs a person.
