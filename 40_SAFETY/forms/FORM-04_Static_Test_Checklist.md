| **More distance available and taken?** | ☐ Yes ☐ No — *there is no penalty for being further away* |

### 3.2 Site characteristics

- [ ] **Clear radius around the pad: 50 m minimum** clear of dry vegetation, combustible material, structures, parked vehicles
- [ ] **No dry grass** within 25 m of the pad — cleared, mown, or wetted
- [ ] **Backstop present:** earth berm or solid wall, at least as tall as the motor axis
- [ ] **No line of sight** from the firing point or any public area to the motor
- [ ] **No overhead** power lines, tree canopy, or structures above the motor
- [ ] **No buried services** within 25 m of the pad — verified with the landowner
- [ ] **Firing point has hard cover** — trench, berm, concrete wall, or a vehicle's engine block, **behind the cover, not beside it**
- [ ] **Access control:** single controlled entry point, or a perimeter Range Safety can physically walk
- [ ] **Two directions of retreat** from the pad, walked and confirmed clear
- [ ] **Bulk water at the pad** — bowser or multiple 20 L drums
- [ ] **Communications:** everyone within earshot of a shout, horn, or radio
- [ ] **Mobile signal verified at this site**, or a vehicle available to reach signal
- [ ] **Nearest hospital** identified; distance and route written on the site sheet
- [ ] **Written landowner permission** on paper, signed, with a validity date, carried on site

### 4.1 The thirteen pre-conditions — tick every one

1. ☐ A **written test plan** exists, is signed by the Safety Officer and Test Director, and has been briefed to every person present
2. ☐ The **motor is the motor that was analysed** — same case, same grain geometry, same nozzle, same propellant batch. **No undocumented changes**
3. ☐ The **case has been proof-tested** hydrostatically to **1.2 × MEOP** (new design)
4. ☐ The grain has **passed the density check and the tap test** (FORM-03 attached) — Grain ID: ____________
5. ☐ The **igniter is sized to the motor** and its charge is recorded — type: ____________ charge: ______ g
6. ☐ **Distance and cover meet the requirement** — **75–100 m proven; 150 m or more for a new design**
7. ☐ **Range Safety has declared the range clear** — out loud, to the Test Director
8. ☐ The **three-item rule** is satisfied **at the pad**: dunk bucket, fire extinguisher, escape route
9. ☐ **Fire cover is present**, correct and in date — at the pad **and** at the firing point
10. ☐ **Every person is accounted for** at the firing point or the observation point. **Nobody downrange**
11. ☐ The **igniter leads are SHUNTED**, and will remain shunted until the moment of connection
12. ☐ **Data capture and video are running and synced**, before arming
13. ☐ **Weather is within limits**

## 12. MISFIRE PROCEDURE — reference box

> ### 🔴 THE CRITICAL RULE
> **If an igniter or motor fails to initiate: WAIT. Then approach cautiously, and ALWAYS DISCONNECT
> THE IGNITER LEADS FIRST.**
> *The motor did not fire. Your instinct is to go and look. That instinct kills people.*

### 12.1 Immediate actions — do not move from cover

1. **Do not release the FIRE button early and do not press it again.** Hold for the full expected burn plus 5 seconds, then release.
2. If there is no ignition within **5 seconds** of the fire command, call **"MISFIRE"** — loudly, so everyone hears.
3. **Switch to SAFE.**
4. **Remove the key** and put it in your pocket.
5. **Disconnect the firing-circuit battery** at the box.
6. **Announce: "SYSTEM SAFE. HOLDING FOR FIVE MINUTES. NOBODY MOVES."**
7. **Everyone stays behind cover.** Nobody stands up to "see better".

### 12.2 The waiting periods — minimums, not targets

| Situation | Minimum wait before anyone approaches |
|---|---|
| Igniter did not initiate; motor clearly unburned, no smoke, no heat | **5 minutes** |
| Motor smoked, sparked, puffed, or partially burned, then stopped | **15 minutes** |
| Motor ignited and burned, and appears to have finished | **10 minutes**, then follow Phase F |
| Anything you do not understand — unusual noise, smell, smoke, or a partial burn | **30 minutes**, and the Safety Officer decides. **If in doubt, wait longer and then wait again** |

**Why the wait is not negotiable:** a slow-burning igniter, a smouldering grain, or a delayed pyrotechnic
reaction can initiate late. Heat-soaked hardware can reignite propellant when air reaches it.
**The wait costs minutes. The alternative costs hands.**

### 12.3 The approach — the order of operations

**Two persons minimum. Safety Officer leads.**

1. Safety Officer announces the approach, **out loud**, so everyone knows
2. **Approach from the side** — never in front of the nozzle, never directly behind the case head
3. **Walk. Do not run.** Watch where you step — debris may be hot or sharp
4. **DISCONNECT THE IGNITER LEADS FIRST — BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE.** Start at the control-point end if the run is long
5. **Then disconnect the igniter from the motor**
6. **Immediately SHUNT the igniter leads** (twist the bared ends together) once they are free
7. **Only then** touch or inspect anything else
8. **Never tug on the igniter wires** — a tug can move the igniter inside the grain or disturb a hot grain
9. **Feel for heat before touching the motor.** If it is warm, **back off and keep waiting**
10. Inspect the igniter: did the bridgewire burn through? Is it intact? Is it still in position?
11. **Record everything** before disturbing the motor: igniter condition, position, motor temperature, time elapsed

### 12.5 Misfire prohibitions

| ❌ Never | Why |
|---|---|
| **Approach before the minimum wait** | A late initiation while your hand is on the motor |
| **Touch the igniter before disconnecting the leads** | The leads are the connection to the power source. **Disconnect first, always** |
| **Send someone to "just have a quick look"** | Same as approaching. The wait applies to everyone |
| **Assume the motor is dead because it looks dead** | Heat-soaked hardware reignites. **Time, not appearance, is the criterion** |
| **Re-fire without a documented cause for the misfire** | You will repeat it, possibly with a partially-burned grain of unknown geometry |
| **Try to pull a partially burned grain out of a case** | It is live propellant. Treat it as such |
| **Skip logging the misfire** | Misfires are data and warnings. Hiding one is a disciplinary matter |
